# Vision

What this app is for, who uses it, and how it should feel. The master of the app's declarations: `personas.md` holds the cast and `permissions.md` the sharing decisions, and this file links to them where the story needs them.

## Purpose

Write this when: the conversation settles what the app is for and who it serves — usually the first turn or two.

## People

Write this when: the cast is named; keep one line per persona here and the full procedure in `personas.md`.

## Tone and values

Write this when: a participant says how the app should feel ("warm and brief", "no gamification"), or corrects a tone you chose.
